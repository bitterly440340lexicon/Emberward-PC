## 🕹️ About

Emberward is a strategic roguelite tower defense game developed and published by Refic Games, released on September 17, 2026. Set across colorful fantasy regions threatened by strange monsters and Void corruption, it lets you build twisting mazes from tetromino-shaped block cards before each battle. Place distinctive towers, collect relics and runes, strengthen your character through talents, and adapt to uniquely generated maps during every run. Adventure, Endless, and Enigma Sanctum modes offer different ways to test your defenses.

A colorful, thoughtful strategy game for players who enjoy tower defense, roguelites, deckbuilding, and creative puzzle-like planning.

## ✨ Features

- 🔥 **Characters and Flames** — Choose different characters and flame types to define each adventure.
- ♾️ **Endless Challenges** — Battle through special event maps and more than 80 stages while chasing leaderboard records.
- 🃏 **Run-Based Deckbuilding** — Collect block cards, towers, relics, and upgrades as each run unfolds.
- 🧩 **Tetromino Maze Building** — Shape enemy routes with limited wall blocks and carefully planned maze layouts.
- 🎯 **Enigma Sanctum** — Create the strongest possible defense while working with a limited selection of cards.
- 🌌 **Void Depths** — Explore a corrupted dungeon filled with portals, crystals, mutated monsters, and shifting routes.
- 👑 **Boss-Focused Adventure** — Progress through regions, overcome challenging stages, and face powerful bosses.
- ✨ **Rune Enhancements** — Add runes to block cards to improve towers or unlock special effects.
- 🏰 **Distinctive Towers** — Deploy towers with unusual sizes, abilities, and tactical roles.
